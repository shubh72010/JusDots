## Overview

Every screen shares the same floating pill — tap to expand, drag to switch tabs, long-press + to create. Notes, maps, checklists and secrets all live in local Room storage; the only network egress is an OpenRouter call you explicitly tap, with your key.

## Features

- Spatial maps with drag, `[[wiki]]` links — not folders
- Markdown blocks, checklists, images via camera or picker
- Biometric secret vault, pinned notes, 30-day trash
