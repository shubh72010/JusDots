## Overview

Built for one thumb, on the move. Rotary dials or Number-dots mode, detent haptics, six colorways — basic arithmetic with parens, done without aiming.

## Features

- Button-free drag input, one-handed friendly
- Word-spelled results, persistent on-device history
- Six colorways, fully offline, no ads or tracking
