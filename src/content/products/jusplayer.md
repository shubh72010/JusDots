## Overview

JusPlayer isn't another generic YouTube Music wrapper. Multiple accounts with quick switching, your playlists and liked songs after sign-in, plus local file playback — with a private, uninterrupted listening experience throughout.

## Features

- Ad-free playback with background listening
- EBU R128 loudness normalization, tempo/pitch/speed controls, crossfade
- Live lyrics, live statistics, theme customization
- Fast startup, lightweight, Kotlin + Compose + Material 3
