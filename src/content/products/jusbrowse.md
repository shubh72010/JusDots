## Overview

Not a giant fork — a well-isolated delta. Mozilla Firefox Android with JusDots branding (`com.jusdots.jusbrowse`), a web-card shell, and nothing phoning home. Pre-alpha: expect rough edges.

## Features

- Web-card shell — pages in an inset rounded card above the toolbar
- 6 palettes (Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Gruvbox, Nord, Dracula, Rosé Pine)
- Second toolbar shortcut slot, top/bottom placement, background toggle
- Patch-stack architecture: bounded, reviewable, rebaseable
